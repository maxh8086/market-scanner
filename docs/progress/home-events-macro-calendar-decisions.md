# Macro and calendar doc: findings needing a user decision

Doc: `docs/designs/home-events-macro-calendar.md`. Review: `docs/progress/home-events-macro-calendar-review.md` (2026-11-10). Storage was decided 2026-11-10 (overview section 6.3: events database, Fastify only). Line numbers below are the doc's original numbers before this edit; the section name is given as well.

The user has not decided any item below. Where the review gives no recommendation, the proposal is marked "proposed by orchestrator draft".

## Decisions

B3. The panel's data has no source (rate action, new rate and stance). Question in plain words: for each rate decision, where do the action (for example a 25 bp cut), the new rate and the stance come from? Options from the review: type them in by hand; store the verbatim source text without classifying it; record the action only; defer; or parse the title. The stance words also need settling, because RBI's own terms (accommodative, neutral, calibrated tightening) do not map onto hawkish, dovish and neutral, and the Fed states no stance in that form. Review's recommendation: the zoom-out lens proposes "decision date, headline, link, verbatim stance text, optional hand-entered action and rate". The review does not decide it. Doc lines affected: 10 (scope), 13 (cut list), 21 to 22 (macro panel), 67 (conflicts).

B4. Collector layout and registration for the macro sources. Question: will macro run as one source or as several (RBI and Fed polling, FRED, the India CPI job)? What cron does each use, and are they registered as events sources? Review's recommendation: no recommendation on the layout. The review asks for a decision. The zoom-out lens recommends the smallest v1, but it framed that for the main database, which the storage decision superseded. Proposed by orchestrator draft: one macro source with one schedule for RBI and Fed, and the India CPI job as a separate source only if M4 keeps the job. Doc lines affected: the new Dependencies section; build specs items 2 and 5; India CPI (36 to 39).

M2. Poll cadence. Question: the overview's default (every 5 minutes from 08:00 to 15:30 IST on trading days, hourly otherwise) misses the macro release times (Fed statement 00:30 IST, US CPI 18:00 or 19:00 IST, India CPI 16:00 IST). Should macro use one low-frequency cron, or windows calendar-driven around each row's time? Should the RSS "default off" and "older than 1 week dropped" rules apply to RBI and Fed? Review's recommendation: none; it lists the two options. Proposed by orchestrator draft: one low-frequency cron for the RBI and Fed feeds, and calendar windows only for the dated releases. Doc lines affected: Cadence and lead times (72 to 73); Carried from the overview (83).

M3. Cooldown rows and per-host request policy. Question: should the split-spec session add cooldown rows for RBI, Federal Reserve, FRED, BLS and MoSPI (as it did for the filings hosts), or should the doc state that no shared cooldown applies? Should the 15-minute retry rule (split spec 4.2) apply? The cap conflict (overview section 7.3 says 3 h; split spec 4.3a says 2 h) is for the split spec to settle. Review's recommendation: "Decide: request new rows from the SS session (FS did this for its hosts), or state that no shared cooldown applies." No pick. Proposed by orchestrator draft: request rows, because a wrong URL can return 403 and trigger a cooldown (M10). Doc lines affected: Rate limits and gaps (75 to 77); Carried from the overview (82).

M4. India CPI job in v1. Question: is the India CPI ingestion job in v1, or is the value entered by hand with the press-release link? Also: what does "the next available slot after the data release" mean, and what is the release-day polling window and the status of a "not yet published" run? Review's recommendation: "zoom-out suggests storing the value by hand with the press-release link in v1." This is the zoom-out lens's suggestion; the review does not decide it. Doc lines affected: India CPI (36 to 39); build specs item 3.

M5. FRED units and key placement. Question: what unit is shown for headline CPI (the index level `CPIAUCSL` is not a rate; the options are YoY percent by `units=pc1` from FRED, or computed here)? How are revisions handled, and observation date versus release date? How is `FEDFUNDS` (a monthly average that lags the FOMC action) used beside decision rows? Is the `FREDAPI_KEY` stored in the app secret store (per `secrets-and-roles.md`) or in `.env`? Review's recommendation: no pick on units or key placement; it asks for "a field list with units and sources" and "Decide secret store versus .env". Proposed by orchestrator draft: display YoY percent; store the key in the app secret store, not `.env`, and register the name. Doc lines affected: FRED (24 to 30), especially the key line (28).

M6. Trading-day calendar. Question: does macro need a trading-day calendar at all? The repo names three sources: the NSE/BSE calendar (overview item 7, the doc's working assumption), the Upstox market-holidays API (filings spec D6b, decided source), and a holiday list in the UI code (per the review, `EXCHANGE_HOLIDAYS_2026` in `ui-v2/src/lib/marketSession.ts`; this was not checked in this session). Review's recommendation: "Suggest dropping stub item 6 and closing OV 8.2 item 7 as 'weekday cron; holidays cost one empty run', or reassigning it to a cross-cutting owner." Doc lines affected: Trading-day calendar (61 to 63); build specs item 6 (95).

M7. Retention for macro and calendar rows. Question: keep these rows (they are small), or keep them for 3 years like event rows? Who runs the purge? Review's recommendation: "Say keep (tiny) or 3 years, and name the purge owner." No pick. Proposed by orchestrator draft: keep them, with no purge in v1; the filings doc owns any later purge. Doc lines affected: Carried from the overview (84).

M8. Which screen shows the panel and calendar. Question: the doc says the calendar is for the "market dashboard" (overview section 10.2), but that is not a screen in `ui-redesign-spec.md` (its section 5.1 is the scan table, section 6 the event rule, and section 7 says market mood is not available). Which screen, which owning UI spec, which endpoints, and what empty and stale states? Review's recommendation: "Name the screen, the owning UI spec, the endpoints, and empty and stale states." No pick. Proposed by orchestrator draft: a macro panel and a calendar block on the Home screen, owned by the UI redesign spec. Doc lines affected: header Screen line (5); Scope (11).

M9. Alert channel for rate decisions and CPI. Question: should there be a dashboard countdown, a push through the existing Telegram path, or no alerts in v1? Lead times are also open. Review's recommendation: zoom-out lens says "cut from v1" as speculative for four dated events a month. The review does not decide it. Doc lines affected: Cadence and lead times (73); build spec items (101 in the original Open list).

M10. Terms of use for RBI, Federal Reserve, BLS and MoSPI. Question: these four hosts have not been read for terms and are not listed in overview section 4.1 or item 15 (unverified). Should they be read and recorded before a fetch, or should the user record personal-use acceptance? Some sites may block scripted clients (unknown). Review's recommendation: "Add them to item 15 or record the user's personal-use acceptance." No pick. Proposed by orchestrator draft: read the terms in a sample-capture step before any fetch, and record the result in overview item 15. Doc lines affected: FRED terms (30); Open list.

M13. Governing copy of the overview's section 10 facts. Question: this doc and overview section 10 both hold the macro and calendar rules. Which one governs, and which becomes a pointer? Also: stance definitions, the trading-day calendar, cadence and rate limits sit under "Owned rules" although they are open; should they move to Open with one owner each? Review's recommendation: "Pick the governing copy and shrink the other to a pointer." No pick. Proposed by orchestrator draft: this doc governs the macro rules (the overview's own ownership table gives the macro series and calendar rows to this doc), and overview section 10 becomes a pointer. Doc lines affected: header (3); Conflicts resolved (65 to 68); Owned rules and Open (98 to 104).

N5. Keyword filter for the RBI and Fed feeds. Question: which list serves the RBI and Fed feed filter (this doc, line 19, versus overview section 9.2, which the filings doc owns); does the filter match the title or the description; whole words or substring; and what does "priority 1" mean now that priorities 2 and 3 are cut? The review notes this filter includes "Stance" and has no "Fed", while the overview's macro prefilter does. Review's recommendation: no recommendation; it asks for the four points to be defined. Proposed by orchestrator draft: one list for these feeds here, matched as whole words in the title and description, with a negative fixture. Doc lines affected: Macro panel keyword filter (19).
